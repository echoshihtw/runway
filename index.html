Know your runway.

Financial Runway is a personal financial runway app for iPhone. It answers one question at all times: how long can your money last?

It is built for people in a defined financial chapter: studying abroad, between jobs, bootstrapping a project, or living off savings. Your financial data stays encrypted on your device. There is no account to create.
