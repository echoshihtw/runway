It is built for people in a defined financial chapter: studying abroad, between jobs, bootstrapping a project, or living off savings. Your records are encrypted on your device and there is no server to read them.

Three screens

A budget you spend down, a log that takes seconds, and a way to try a change before you make it.

A budget is a cap, not a cost

Logging a coffee uses up your living budget instead of adding to your monthly costs, so the number only moves when you go over. That is the one rule the whole figure rests on, and the app says so on the screen where it applies.
