Stop guessing how long your money lasts.

One number on your iPhone: how many months your money covers if income paused today, and the month it would run out. No account. No bank connection.

It is built for people in a defined financial chapter: studying abroad, between jobs, bootstrapping a project, or living off savings. Your records are encrypted on your device and there is no server to read them, because setting it up is a balance and a budget rather than a bank login.

Runway Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. An app that counts what your subscriptions cost you has no business being one of them.
